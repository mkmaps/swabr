# Storage

Storage shows where your space goes – like an X-ray of your disk.

## Overview
The start page measures the main places: Home (without Library), Library, Applications, system Library and typical space hogs such as iOS backups, old downloads (older than 90 days), Xcode and Docker data.

## Using it
- **Double-click** opens a folder; the path bar at the top jumps back.
- **List or tiles:** switch at the top right. The tile view (treemap) shows sizes as areas.
- **Largest files:** the document icon in the toolbar shows the 20 largest files.
- **Quick Look:** the space bar shows a preview.
- **Check boxes** select items for the Trash.

## Markers
- **"removable":** build or dependency folders (e.g. node_modules) that are recreated.
- **Size with "+":** at least this big; some items were unreadable (Full Disk Access?) or measuring took longer than the time limit (30 seconds, adjustable).

## What happens when deleting?
Selected items go to the Trash and can be put back. The review window warns about app data, iCloud folders and media libraries. System folders, your home folder itself and running VM data are locked.

## Notes
- iCloud placeholders are not downloaded and count with their local size (often almost 0).
- Other drives are not included.
