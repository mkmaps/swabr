# Installers

Installers looks for installation files that are usually unnecessary after installing: .dmg, .pkg, .mpkg, .iso, .xip and zip archives containing an app.

## Searched places
Downloads, Desktop, Documents, Public, Library/Downloads, the Homebrew cache, iCloud Downloads, Mail attachments and Telegram (up to 2 levels deep). The App Store edition searches Downloads only.

## Afterwards
Selected files go to the Trash. Installed programs stay; to reinstall, download the installer again. The space bar or a right-click shows a preview.
