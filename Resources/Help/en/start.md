# Getting started

Swabr swabs your Mac's deck clean: it removes data you don't need, uninstalls apps completely, shows where your storage goes and keeps an eye on your Mac's health.

## The sections
| Section | What for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your Mac's health and how much space can be freed, every area at a glance |
| **Clean Up** | Remove caches, logs, leftovers and developer caches |
| **Apps** | Uninstall apps with their leftovers, find updates, manage startup items |
| **Optimize** | Maintenance for Finder, network, databases and macOS services |
| **Storage** | See which folders and files take how much space |
| **Status** | CPU, memory, disk, network, battery and a health score |
| **Projects** | Remove build folders such as node_modules or target from your projects |
| **Installers** | Find old .dmg, .pkg and zip installers |
| **Slim Down** | Remove Intel code your Mac doesn't need |
| **History** | Look up everything Swabr removed |

## How to work with it
- **Start with the overview:** "Check everything" searches **Clean Up**, **Projects**, **Installers** and **Slim Down** at once. Then "Review & clean up" takes you to the area with the most space.
- **Look first, decide later:** every section only scans at first. Nothing is deleted until you have checked the list and confirmed the review window.
- **Read the effects:** every row says what happens after removal. Look closer at entries marked orange.
- **Help in place:** the question-mark button at the top right opens the matching help page.

> Tip: the Direct edition needs **Full Disk Access** once, otherwise Trash, Mail and some app data stay invisible. See "Full Disk Access".
