# Status and menu bar

## Health score (0–100)
Points are taken off for:
| Area | Weight | From |
|---|---|---|
| CPU load | 30 | above 50 %, strongly above 85 % |
| Memory | 25 | above 70 %, strongly above 88 %; plus memory pressure |
| Disk | 20 | above 80 % full, strongly above 93 % |
| Temperature | 15 | above 65 °C (if measurable) |
| Input/output | 10 | above 50 MB/s |
| Battery | up to 5 | above 800 cycles or below 80 % capacity |
| Uptime | up to 3 | more than 7 or 14 days without restart |

85–100 excellent, 65–84 good, 45–64 fair, below that your Mac needs attention.

## Tiles
CPU (total, load, per core), memory (used, available, compressed, swap, pressure), disk (usage, read/write), power (charge, condition, cycles), network (down/up, connection, proxy) and the busiest processes. Measured every 2 seconds (adjustable).

## Menu bar
The boat at the top right shows a live value (choose: health score, CPU, CPU+RAM or icon only). Hovering opens the mini dashboard, a click keeps it open. The animation plays at launch and when opening. Switch it on or off under Settings → General.
